The basics

What Shopify and Salesforce integration gives you

It is an automatic exchange of customer, order and engagement data between your Shopify store and Salesforce CRM. A rep opening an account sees the full Shopify order history and lifetime value without switching tabs, and a support agent working a case sees the exact order involved, its fulfilment status and any earlier returns.

Shopify knows what was bought, when and for how much. Salesforce knows who the customer is to you, what they have been told and which deals or cases are open. Kept apart, those two views leave B2B and high-touch DTC teams deciding on half the facts, or asking buyers to explain everything again.

Systems apart

  • Order history exported to CSV
  • Support blind to the order in question
  • High-value carts go unnoticed
  • Segments out of step across teams

Systems linked

  • Orders and LTV on the record
  • Cases tied to the exact order
  • Big carts flagged for follow-up
  • Shared segments for sales and marketing

Choosing a route

Four ways to connect Shopify to Salesforce

The best route depends on whether you run Sales Cloud, Service Cloud or Commerce Cloud, and on how live your sales team needs the data to be. As far as we have seen there is no single official Shopify to Salesforce connector, so the link normally runs through a package, an API build or middleware.

RouteWhat it isSuitsKeep in mind
Commerce Cloud toolingNative tools for when Salesforce itself runs commerce features.Teams already invested in the Salesforce commerce ecosystemOverkill if you only want CRM visibility, not a storefront
AppExchange sync appPrebuilt packages that sync Shopify orders into Sales or Service Cloud.Standard customer and order visibility, live quicklyA recurring fee, and the package's own object mapping
REST or Bulk API plus webhooksA custom build using both platforms' APIs directly.Custom objects, complex territory or approval logicNeeds ongoing Apex or Salesforce admin support
MiddlewareOrchestration in Make, n8n or code, joining Shopify, Salesforce and more.Multi-system flows, such as Shopify, Salesforce and an ERPMore moving parts to watch after launch

If all you need is order history and LTV on the Contact record, an AppExchange app is usually quicker and cheaper than custom Apex. We will tell you so even when it means less build work for us.
