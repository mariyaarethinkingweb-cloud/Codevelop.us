The basics

What this project actually covers

Moving a sales team off Salesforce is rarely a single cutover weekend. This work handles both situations: a one-time migration of accounts, contacts, deals and activities into Zoho CRM, and an ongoing sync that keeps both systems current while the team transitions in phases.

We map every custom field and object before we touch production data, so the records that arrive in Zoho keep the same structure, and the same history, your sales team relies on in Salesforce today.

A rushed migration

  • Custom fields flattened or dropped
  • Activity history left behind
  • Duplicate accounts after the move
  • Reps unsure which system to trust

A mapped migration

  • Custom fields carried across intact
  • Calls, emails and tasks preserved
  • Record counts validated before go-live
  • One source of truth at every stage
Cutover path

From Salesforce to Zoho in five safe steps

  1. Audit

    We inventory your objects, custom fields, data volume and the target Zoho setup.

  2. Map

    Every field and object is matched to its Zoho equivalent on a signed sheet.

  3. Dry run

    A full practice migration into a sandbox, with record counts checked.

  4. Cutover

    Live data moves across, in one go or team by team.

  5. Parallel sync

    Optionally, both systems stay current until the last team switches.

Choosing a route

Four ways to build it, and when each one fits

Salesforce has mature REST, SOAP and Bulk APIs, while Zoho offers REST APIs and Deluge on the receiving side. A phased move usually puts a temporary sync on top of a one-time bulk import.

Salesforce Bulk APIHigh-volume batch export

Best when you're doing a one-time migration of a large data set.

Trade-off fields must be mapped carefully before the export.

Salesforce REST APISmaller or incremental pulls

Best when you need ongoing sync during a phased cutover.

Trade-off call limits on lower Salesforce editions.

Zoho REST API and DelugeImports on the Zoho side

Best when you're loading migrated records into Zoho CRM cleanly.

Trade-off Deluge alone isn't built for very large bulk loads.

Custom middlewareA temporary sync layer

Best when both CRMs must stay current while teams migrate gradually.

Trade-off you need a partner who can maintain it during the transition.

A rushed migration is the biggest cause of lost deal history. We map every custom field and object first, then migrate, even when that means a slightly longer timeline.
