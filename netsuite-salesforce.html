NetSuite and Salesforce, from signed deal to paid invoice without re-keying.

We connect Salesforce, where your reps sell, to NetSuite, where your finance team bills and collects. A closed deal becomes a sales order on its own, and fulfilment, invoice and payment status flow back to the account so sales never has to ask finance where an order stands.

Book a scoping call See budget and timing
The problem

Sales owns the deal, finance owns the money, and neither sees the other

Salesforce runs your pipeline. NetSuite runs billing, inventory and cash. Without a link, someone retypes every won deal into the ERP, and reps ping finance to find out whether a customer has been shipped to or has paid.

Join them up and the handoff disappears. Customer records stay aligned, approved quotes become sales orders, and the account page in Salesforce shows fulfilment, invoices and balances, so the people closest to the customer have the facts when they call.

Not connected

  • Won deals retyped into NetSuite
  • Duplicate customers in each system
  • Reps email finance for order status
  • Delivery dates promised to customers on credit hold

Connected

  • Closed-won creates the sales order
  • One matched customer record
  • Status visible on the account
  • Credit holds flagged before a promise is made

In practice

What each side sees once it is live

The mapping sheet you sign off lists every field. These are the areas most teams care about first.

Customer records

  • External ID
  • Addresses
  • Terms

Names, addresses, contacts and payment terms stay aligned, and a matching rule stops a returning customer being created twice.

Sales orders

  • Lines
  • Pricing
  • Subsidiary

Items, quantities, agreed prices and the right subsidiary or currency carry across, so finance receives an order ready to process.

Catalogue and prices

  • Items
  • Price books
  • Discounts

Reps quote from the same products and price levels the ERP will bill, which cuts down corrections after the deal closes.

Fulfilment status

  • Ship date
  • Tracking
  • Backorders

Ship dates and tracking numbers appear on the Salesforce account, so reps can answer "where is my order" in one look.

Invoices and balances

  • Invoices
  • Balance
  • Payments

Sales and customer success can see what is owed and what has been paid before a renewal or upsell conversation.

Credit control

  • Limits
  • Holds
  • Releases

A credit hold shows up on the account straight away, so nobody promises a delivery that finance will have to block.

We also handle custom objects, multi-subsidiary rules and multi-currency pricing, which is where templated connectors usually need extra work.

Choosing a route

Four ways to build the link, and when each fits

Both platforms have capable APIs, so the real choice is how the flow is orchestrated, who maintains it and how much custom logic it carries.

NetSuite SuiteTalk RESTModern REST access to records

Best when you are starting fresh, using standard records or posting in real time.

Trade-off governance limits can bite during high-volume bursts.

RESTlets and SuiteScriptCustom endpoints inside NetSuite

Best when records are non-standard or heavy transformation is needed.

Trade-off the code lives in NetSuite and needs ongoing upkeep.

Salesforce REST and Bulk APIRecord-level, batch and Platform Events

Best when you trigger on close or move large volumes of accounts.

Trade-off API call limits on lower Salesforce editions.

iPaaS connectorsCeligo, Boomi, Workato and similar

Best when a standard opportunity-to-order flow needs to go live quickly.

Trade-off a continuing subscription, and templates have limits.

For a simple flow of opportunity, to sales order, to invoice status, a ready-made connector is often enough. Custom middleware earns its cost when you have approval logic, several subsidiaries or non-standard records, and we will tell you honestly which side of that line you are on.

How we work

From first call to monitored go-live

Every project follows the same five stages, and you approve the scope and the field mapping before anything is built.

  1. Discover

    We map Salesforce objects, NetSuite records and the system of record for each.

  2. Map

    Every field, direction, matching rule and error case is written down for sign-off.

  3. Build

    We develop in a NetSuite sandbox and a Salesforce sandbox, never on live data.

  4. Test

    Real deals end to end, including amendments, cancellations, duplicates and failures.

  5. Support

    Logging, alerts and a named contact after go-live.
