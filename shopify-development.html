What is Shopify, and where does AI fit in?

Shopify is the e-commerce platform built for selling online. Add AI on top and every part of the store gets smarter. Explore the seven building blocks below.

AI Store Builder

Launch responsive, brand-matched stores faster, with AI-assisted product copy, imagery, and layouts refined by our designers.

Smart Inventory & Forecasting

Manage products and stock with demand forecasting that helps you reorder before bestsellers run out.

Secure, Flexible Payments

AI Search & SEO

App & AI Agent Ecosystem
