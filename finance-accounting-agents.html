How it works

Safely live in your finance stack in four steps.

  1. 1

    Connect accounting and banking

    Link your accounting software, bank feeds, inbox and chat with the permissions you choose. Start read-only if you prefer.

  2. 2

    Teach it your rules

    We load your chart of accounts, coding habits, approval limits and vendor rules, so the agent works the way your team does.

  3. 3

    Run it beside your team

    For a few weeks the agent suggests while your team decides. We compare results and tune accuracy before anything posts on its own.

  4. Go live with approvals and logs

    Routine work runs automatically, anything unusual waits for a person, and every action is logged for your auditors.
