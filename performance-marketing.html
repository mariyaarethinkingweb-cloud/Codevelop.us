Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about how performance marketing works with Codevelop.

Performance marketing is a results-driven approach where every campaign is tied to measurable business outcomes such as leads, sales, or customer acquisition. For Portland-based businesses, it ensures that your marketing budget is focused on strategies that deliver tangible ROI, rather than generic impressions or clicks.

At Codevelop, we define success through key performance indicators (KPIs) aligned with your business goals. This includes metrics like conversion rate, cost per lead, revenue generated, and overall return on ad spend (ROAS). Every campaign is tracked, optimized, and reported with transparency.

The right channels depend on your target audience and business objectives. Common channels include:

  • Paid search (Google Ads, Bing)
  • Paid social (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok)
  • Programmatic display and retargeting
  • Email marketing and automation

Codevelop develops a custom channel mix for each client, based on audience insights and historical performance.

We continuously monitor and optimize campaigns using a data-driven approach. This includes adjusting bids, refining targeting, and testing creative strategies. By focusing on high-value audiences and measurable outcomes, we minimize waste and maximize the efficiency of your marketing spend.

Yes. Our team ensures that campaigns are fully integrated with your CRM, analytics platforms, and marketing tools. This allows for seamless data flow, accurate reporting, and coordinated strategy execution across all digital touchpoints.

Performance marketing delivers both short-term and long-term impact. Initial improvements in lead generation or traffic can often be seen within weeks, while strategic optimizations over time improve ROI and campaign efficiency. At Codevelop, we provide transparent reporting so you can track progress in real time.
