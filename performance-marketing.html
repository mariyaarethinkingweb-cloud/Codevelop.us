Straight answers about how AI-powered performance marketing works with Codevelop.

AI performance marketing ties every campaign to measurable outcomes such as leads, sales, or customer acquisition, then uses machine learning to improve those results continuously. For Portland businesses, it means your budget is steered toward the audiences, keywords and creatives most likely to deliver real ROI.

AI analyzes far more signals than a human team can, including audience behavior, bids, creatives and timing. It predicts what will convert and adjusts campaigns automatically, while our strategists set direction, review results and keep the work on brand.

We define success through KPIs aligned with your business goals, such as conversion rate, cost per lead, revenue generated, and return on ad spend (ROAS). Every campaign is tracked, optimized by AI, and reported with full transparency.

The right mix depends on your audience and objectives. Common channels include:

  • Paid search (Google Ads, Bing)
  • Paid social (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok)
  • Programmatic display and predictive retargeting
  • Email marketing and AI-driven automation

Codevelop builds a custom channel mix for each client, using audience insights and historical performance data.

Our AI monitors campaigns around the clock, adjusting bids, pausing weak ad sets, refining targeting and testing new creatives. By focusing spend on high-value audiences, we minimize waste and maximize the efficiency of every dollar.
