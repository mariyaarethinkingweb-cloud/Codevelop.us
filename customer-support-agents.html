How it works

From first call to live agent in four steps.

  1. 1

    Audit your support

    We review your tickets, channels and tools to find what’s repetitive and what must stay human.

  2. 2

    Connect and train

    Plug in your helpdesk, store and CRM. The agent learns from your help centre, policies and past replies.

  3. 3

    Test, then launch

    It starts in draft mode with your team approving replies. Once accuracy is proven, it goes live.

  4. 4

    Review and improve

    Weekly reports show what it solved and missed. We tighten answers and add new skills over time.
