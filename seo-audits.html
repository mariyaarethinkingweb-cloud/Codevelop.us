What we check

Technical SEO

We check that your site is fast, mobile-friendly, and easy for search engines to crawl, so your content can actually be found.

  • Crawling and indexing
  • Site speed
  • Mobile usability
  • Structured data
  • Broken links and redirects

With AICrawl data is reviewed at scale and issues are ranked by impact, so the most valuable fixes come first.

On-page SEO

We review how each page is built so search engines and visitors understand what it is about.

  • Titles and meta descriptions
  • Headings
  • Internal linking
  • Image alt text
  • Duplicate pages

With AISite-wide scans catch missing and duplicate elements in minutes, and our team reviews each one.

Content and search intent

We check whether your pages answer the questions customers ask and give them a clear next step.

  • Search intent match
  • Thin or outdated pages
  • Gaps against competitors
  • Clear answers and calls to action

With AISearch questions are grouped by intent, so we can see which topics you are missing.

Local SEO

We check how you appear in Portland and nearby areas, in Google Maps and in local search.

  • Google Business Profile
  • Citation consistency
  • Reviews
  • City and service-area pages

With AICompetitor profiles and mismatched listings are compared quickly, then checked by a person.

Backlinks

We look at who links to you, who links to your competitors, and whether any links put you at risk.

  • Backlink profile
  • Risky or low-quality links
  • Competitor link gaps
  • Relevant local sources

With AIProspects are shortlisted by relevance and risky links are flagged for our team to review.
