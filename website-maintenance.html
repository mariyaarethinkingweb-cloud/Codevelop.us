What we maintain

Everything your website needs to stay healthy

Site speed, security, and reliability directly affect user experience and SEO. Pick a area below to see what we do, and what AI adds to it.

Updates & Patching

Outdated software is the most common way sites break or get compromised. We keep your CMS, plugins, themes, and integrations current without surprises.

  • Core, plugin, theme, and platform updates
  • Testing on a staging copy before anything goes live
  • Quick rollback if something misbehaves
  • Compatibility checks across your integrations

With AIUpdate notes are scanned automatically, so risky changes are flagged and reviewed by an engineer first.

Security & Monitoring

Security protects your customers, your reputation, and your search rankings. We watch your site every hour of the day and respond when something looks wrong.

  • Malware scans and firewall protection
  • SSL, login, and user-permission checks
  • Uptime monitoring with instant alerts
  • Clean-up and recovery if an incident happens

With AIUnusual traffic and login patterns are spotted early, so threats are handled before they become outages.

Speed & Performance

A slow site loses visitors and rankings. We keep performance tuned as your content, traffic, and plugins grow.

  • Core Web Vitals and page-speed reviews
  • Image, script, and caching optimization
  • Database and server clean-up
  • Mobile responsiveness checks

With AIThe slowest pages are ranked by impact, so we fix what moves the needle first.

Backups & Recovery

If something goes wrong, you should be back online in minutes, not days. We make sure a good copy of your site always exists.

  • Automated, off-site backups
  • Regular restore tests, not just copies
  • Fast recovery and rollback plans
  • Content and database protection

With AIEvery backup is checked for completeness, and failed jobs raise an alert right away.

SEO & Content Care

Technical issues quietly erode traffic. We keep your site clean for search engines so your marketing keeps paying off.

  • Broken links, redirects, and sitemap upkeep
  • Technical SEO and indexing checks
  • Meta data and structured data review
  • Content updates and small page edits

With AIMissing titles, crawl errors, and dead links are caught automatically and queued for a fix.

Support & Improvements

How AI helps

AI watches. Our engineers decide.

AI does the constant checking and sorting. People review, fix, and approve. That is how you get speed without losing control.

    Watch

    Uptime, speed, errors, and security signals are checked continuously, day and night.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    AI groups alerts, filters the noise, and points to the most likely cause.

  3. 03

    Fix

    An engineer reviews the issue, applies the fix, and tests it on staging first.

  4. 04
