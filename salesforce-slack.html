Salesforce and Slack, so your pipeline updates itself where your team talks.

We wire Salesforce into Slack so deal movement, approvals and escalated cases show up in the right channel, and reps can fix a record without opening the CRM. The result is data that stays current because updating it takes seconds, not a context switch.

The problem

Deals get discussed in Slack and logged nowhere

Most revenue conversations already happen in Slack: a stuck negotiation, a discount ask, an angry customer. Salesforce only learns about them if someone remembers to log in and type it up, and usually nobody does. Forecasts then run on notes that are weeks old.

Connect the two and the CRM comes to the conversation. Stage changes and big wins land in the right channel, a manager approves a quote with one click, and a rep updates an opportunity from a pop-up form that saves straight to the record.

Apart

  • Updates wait for a CRM login
  • Approvals sit in someone's inbox
  • Escalated cases spread by DM
  • Forecast built on stale fields

Connected

  • Changes post to the right channel
  • Approve or reject in one click
  • A swarm channel opens for each escalation
  • Records edited from Slack stay current
What it does

Choosing a route

Four ways to build it, and when each fits

Salesforce and Slack both offer building blocks. The right mix depends on how much you need to write back, how fast alerts must arrive and who will maintain the result.

Official Salesforce for Slack appSalesforce's own Slack app

Best when you mainly want record lookup, simple notifications and channel linking.

Trade-off little room to customise, and write-backs are limited.

Custom Slack appBuilt on Slack's Bolt framework

Best when you need approvals, pop-up forms, slash commands or any bespoke interaction.

Trade-off you take on hosting, authentication and distribution.

Platform EventsEvent-driven, over the Streaming API

Best when alerts must fire the moment a record changes.

Trade-off event and delivery limits apply, and something has to subscribe to the stream.

Flow with outbound messagesDeclarative Salesforce automation

Best when you want simple alerting with no code.

Trade-off less control over formatting, batching and retries.

The official app is often enough for lookup and basic notices. Once you want structured approvals or write-backs, a custom app usually pays for itself, and we will say so plainly if it will not.
