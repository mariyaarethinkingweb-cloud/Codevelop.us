The basics

What this integration actually does

A third-party warehouse can only perform as well as the data it's given. When orders arrive by email or CSV, shipments confirm a day late, cut lines go unnoticed, and NetSuite's stock on hand slowly drifts away from reality.

This integration sends fulfilment requests and inbound receipts to your 3PL or WMS, then brings shipment confirmations, with carton and tracking detail, back into NetSuite item fulfilments. It also reconciles adjustments and cycle counts, so your on-hand quantities stay trustworthy.

Without integration

  • Orders emailed or dropped as CSV files
  • Shipments confirmed a day late
  • Cut lines nobody notices
  • On-hand stock that drifts from the shelf

With integration

  • Orders released to the floor automatically
  • Confirmations arrive as they ship
  • Short ships flagged straight away
  • Stock counts that stay in step
Release to delivery

One order, five handoffs, no emailed spreadsheets

  1. Order released

    NetSuite sends the sales order with ship method and priority.

  2. Warehouse picks

    The 3PL or WMS picks, packs and labels the order.

  3. Shipment confirmed

    Cartons, weights and tracking numbers come back automatically.

  4. Fulfilment posted

    NetSuite creates the item fulfilment, which triggers notification and invoicing.

  5. Stock reconciled

    On-hand snapshots and adjustments keep quantities aligned.

Choosing a route

Four ways to connect a warehouse, and when each one fits

Your provider decides the transport, not us. Most support one of these four, and several support more than one.

Provider REST APIModern JSON from the 3PL or WMS

Best when you want orders released and confirmed in real time.

Trade-off rate limits and provider-specific quirks in the spec.

EDI 940 and 945Shipping order and shipping advice

Best when your 3PL already trades over EDI.

Trade-off it needs a translator and a partner testing cycle.

SFTP flat filesScheduled CSV or fixed-width drops

Best when the provider has no API or you move very high batch volumes.

Trade-off there's latency between drops, and silent failures if unmonitored.

RESTlets and SuiteTalkThe NetSuite side

Best when you're posting fulfilments, receipts and adjustments back accurately.

Trade-off governance limits apply during large inventory snapshots.

These projects rarely go wrong on the connection. They go wrong on exceptions: short ships, cut lines, split shipments and damaged returns. We map those cases explicitly before writing anything.
