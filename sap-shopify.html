The basics

What a Shopify integration actually does

It is an automatic, two-way exchange of data between your Shopify store and the other software you run the business on. A paid order becomes an ERP sales order, an invoice is drafted in your books, and a courier's tracking number returns to the customer, all within moments instead of at the end of the day.

Few growing stores live on Shopify alone. There is usually an ERP or accounting tool, a CRM, a warehouse or 3PL, a marketplace or two and an email platform. Each one is another place where stock drifts, orders get retyped and the month-end close slips. Joining them gives you one consistent picture, and lets order volume climb without the back office growing at the same pace.

Choosing a route

Five ways to connect Shopify to other software

Shopify gives you the Admin API (GraphQL and REST), webhooks for live events, the Storefront API for custom buying experiences, Shopify Flow for simple automations, and a market of iPaaS connectors that package those APIs. Which one fits depends on your volumes, how live the data must be and how standard your process is.

RouteWhat it isSuitsKeep in mind
Admin GraphQL APIShopify's main API. Ask for exactly the fields you need in one request.Custom builds, high volumes, newer Shopify featuresCost-based rate limits and the need for real developer skill
Admin REST APIThe older, resource-style interface to the same store data.Existing apps and quick reads of simple resourcesShopify now treats it as legacy and points new work to GraphQL
WebhooksShopify pushes a notification when an order, fulfilment or stock level changes.Live, event-driven updates to other systemsNeeds retries, duplicate handling and signature checks
iPaaS connectorsPrebuilt templates on platforms such as Celigo, Alumio or Zapier.Standard flows (store to ERP, store to books) that must launch quicklyA subscription that never ends, and templates that only bend so far
Custom middlewarePurpose-built orchestration in Make, n8n or code, using the APIs above.Unusual rules, many systems, AI-assisted automationNeeds a partner who can look after it afterwards

A prebuilt connector is the correct answer for plenty of standard requirements, and when it is we will say so, then help you pick and configure it. We build custom only when your process genuinely calls for it.

Our process

From first call to a monitored live sync

Every project runs through the same five stages. You get a fixed-price scope and a mapping sheet to sign off before anything is built.

  1. Discover

    We list your systems, the records involved, your order volumes and the outcome you are after.

  2. Map

    Every field, direction, transformation and error rule goes on the sheet before a line is configured.

  3. Build

    We build on the chosen platform or codebase, in a sandbox, against a Shopify development store.

  4. Test

    Real order scenarios run end to end, including failures, retries and duplicate orders.

  5. Support

    After go-live we watch the flows with logging and alerts, and you have one named contact.
