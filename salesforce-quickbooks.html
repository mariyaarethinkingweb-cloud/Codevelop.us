A won deal in Salesforce should end up as an invoice in QuickBooks.

We connect your CRM to your books so a closed-won opportunity creates the invoice by itself, and so reps can see what has been billed and paid without asking finance. It works with QuickBooks Online and with Desktop.

The problem

Why sales and finance keep interrupting each other

Salesforce holds the deal and QuickBooks holds the money. With no link, someone retypes each won deal as an invoice, and every "has this been paid yet?" from a rep becomes a message that pulls finance away from their work.

The integration keeps customer records matched, turns closed-won opportunities and approved quotes into QuickBooks invoices, and writes invoice, balance and payment status back to the Salesforce account. Reps can answer their own questions, and finance can stop being the help desk.

Your QuickBooks edition shapes the design. QuickBooks Online has a cloud REST API, so flows can run close to real time. QuickBooks Desktop has no cloud API and is reached through the Web Connector on a polling schedule, which makes Desktop syncs behave more like batches than instant updates.

Systems apart

  • Won deals retyped as invoices
  • Reps asking finance who has paid
  • Duplicate customers in both tools
  • Overdue accounts sold to again

Systems linked

  • Invoices created from closed-won deals
  • Paid, partial and overdue status on the account
  • One matched customer record
  • Overdue balances flagged before the next deal

After go-live

One won deal, from signature to settled invoice

Once the link is running, a typical deal passes between the two systems like this, and nobody retypes it.

  1. Deal won

    A rep closes the opportunity, and a match check looks for an existing QuickBooks customer before anything is created.

  2. Invoice created

    QuickBooks receives an invoice with the correct lines, tax and payment terms, and the invoice number returns to Salesforce.

  3. Invoice visible

    The amount, due date and PDF link appear on the account, so sales and customer success can see what was billed.

  4. Payment recorded

    When finance records a payment, the status moves to paid or partly paid and the open balance updates in the CRM.

  5. Overdue flagged

    If an invoice goes past due, the account shows it, so the rep knows before they send the next proposal.

Choosing a route

How we build the QuickBooks link

Salesforce's APIs are strong, so the QuickBooks edition decides most of the architecture.

QuickBooks Online APIA cloud REST API secured with OAuth 2.0.Modern QBO builds with near real-time invoicesRate limits apply, and the app needs review before go-live
Web Connector (Desktop, QBXML)An Intuit tool that swaps QBXML with the local company file.Reaching QuickBooks Desktop from a cloud CRMRuns on a polling schedule, from the host machine
iPaaS connectorsPrebuilt templates on tools such as Celigo, Workato or Zapier.Standard opportunity-to-invoice flows that must launch fastThe subscription never ends, and templates only bend so far

An honest note: if your flow is simply opportunity to invoice to payment status on QuickBooks Online, a prebuilt connector can be the right answer, and we will help you set it up. Custom middleware earns its cost when Desktop, several company files or approval rules are involved.
