The basics

What Shopify integration actually means

It means your Shopify store and the rest of your software swap data on their own: orders, stock levels, products, customers and shipments. A checkout becomes a NetSuite sales order, a QuickBooks invoice appears the moment payment clears, and a ship confirmation reaches the buyer as a tracking email, all within seconds.

A growing merchant almost never runs on Shopify alone. There's an ERP or accounting tool, a CRM, a warehouse, a marketplace or two and an email platform, and each one is a place where stock drifts and orders get retyped. Connect them and you handle more orders without hiring more people to move data around.

Without integration

  • Orders retyped into the ERP
  • Stock counts that drift between channels
  • Tracking numbers pasted in by hand
  • Finance closing the month late

With integration

  • Checkout creates the order everywhere
  • Live stock on every channel
  • Tracking flows back to the buyer
  • Invoices and payouts reconcile themselves
Checkout to doorstep

One order, five handoffs, nothing re-keyed

  1. Order placed

    A shopper checks out on your Shopify store.

  2. ERP order created

    The ERP receives the customer, items, prices and tax.

  3. Invoice raised

    Accounting issues the invoice from the same order.

  4. Warehouse ships

    The 3PL or shipping tool picks, packs and labels it.

  5. Tracking sent back

    Shopify and your CRM update, and the buyer gets the tracking link.

Choosing a route

Five ways to connect Shopify, and when each one fits

Shopify offers the Admin API in GraphQL and REST, webhooks for live events, the Storefront API, the built-in Shopify Flow tool, and iPaaS connectors that wrap these in templates. Your data volume, how real-time you need to be and how standard your process is decide the best fit.

Admin GraphQL APIShopify's main API

Best when you're building something custom, handling high volumes or using new Shopify features.

Trade-off cost-based rate limits, and it takes development skill.

Admin REST APIThe older resource-based API

Best when an existing app relies on it or you need quick reads of simple data.

Trade-off Shopify now treats it as legacy and steers new work to GraphQL.

WebhooksEvent notifications pushed to you

Best when downstream systems must react to orders, fulfilments or stock changes right away.

Trade-off you must handle retries, duplicates and verification.

iPaaS connectorsCeligo, Alumio or Zapier templates

Best when your flow is standard, such as Shopify to ERP or accounting.

Trade-off the subscription never ends, and templates only bend so far.

Custom middlewareMake, n8n or code

Best when several systems, unusual rules or AI automation are involved.

Trade-off you need a partner to look after it.

For many standard Shopify flows a ready-made connector is the right call. We'll tell you when it is, help you set it up, and build custom only when your process needs it.
