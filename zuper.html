The basics

What Zuper customization actually means

It means setting up Zuper's fields, dashboards and automation to match how your business operates, instead of bending your process around a default setup. Most of it needs no code at all: workflows, checklists, custom fields, dashboard widgets and proposal layouts are all built from the settings panels in the Zuper web app.

When a requirement outgrows those panels, the Developer Hub adds custom functions and API access, so you can automate rules, build reports or trigger actions the standard workflow engine wasn't designed for. The right mix depends on how standard your process is and how much logic sits behind it.

Choosing a route

Four ways to customize Zuper, and when each one fits

Customization runs on no-code settings, with the Developer Hub and APIs ready for requirements the settings can't express. How standard your process is, and how much logic sits behind it, decides where to stop.

No-code settingsDrag-and-drop configuration

Best when your setup is standard and you want the fastest go-live.

Trade-off there's a ceiling on how complex the logic can get.

Developer Hub functionsLow-code automation rules

Best when business rules don't fit the standard triggers.

Trade-off every function needs testing before it touches live jobs.

REST API and webhooksDirect access to jobs, customers, assets

Best when you need custom reporting or triggers from outside systems.

Trade-off it needs developer time, yours or ours.

Combined buildSettings, functions and API together

Best when the work is multi-step or AI-driven.

Trade-off you need a partner who can maintain it.

Zuper's no-code settings are already in your subscription and cover most needs. We'll tell you when they're enough, configure them properly, and reach for code only when your requirement goes past them.
